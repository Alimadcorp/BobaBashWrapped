THE PERSON BEHIND THE NAME

Biography

A Life Beyond Cricket

Imran Ahmed Khan Niazi was born on 5 October 1952 in Lahore, Pakistan. He first became internationally famous as a cricketer and later became known for his charitable work and political career.

His public life has included sporting achievements, the establishment of healthcare and education initiatives, and leadership in Pakistani politics.

His career has made him a significant and often debated figure in Pakistan's modern history.

THE SPORTING YEARS

A Cricket Legend

A fast bowler, an all-rounder and the captain who led Pakistan to its first Cricket World Cup title.

1971–1992

Leading Pakistan on the World Stage

Imran Khan made his Test debut in 1971 and became one of Pakistan's leading all-rounders. His pace bowling, batting and captaincy helped shape an important era in Pakistani cricket.

In 1992, he captained Pakistan to victory in the Cricket World Cup in Australia and New Zealand. The win remains a defining moment in Pakistan's sporting history.

1992

Cricket World Cup Champions

Pakistan won its first men's Cricket World Cup under his captaincy.

SERVICE AND SOCIAL WORK

Philanthropy

A Focus on Healthcare and Education

After retiring from international cricket, Imran Khan helped establish the Shaukat Khanum Memorial Cancer Hospital and Research Centre, which opened in Lahore in 1994.

The hospital was founded in memory of his mother, Shaukat Khanum, and works to provide cancer treatment, including financial support for patients who cannot afford the full cost.

He also founded Namal, an educational initiative that developed into a university in Mianwali.

A CAREER IN PUBLIC SERVICE

Political Journey

His move from sport and charity into electoral politics reshaped his public career.

1996

Founded Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf

He established PTI and began his formal political career.

2002

Entered the National Assembly

He was elected to Pakistan's National Assembly for the first time.

2013

Expanded Political Support

PTI emerged as a major political force in the general election.

2018

Became Prime Minister

Following the general election, he became Pakistan's 22nd prime minister.

2022

Left Office

He was removed from office after losing a vote of no confidence in the National Assembly.
