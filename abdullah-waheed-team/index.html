"Return to Monke. Modern life was a mistake."

-Monkeys are nature’s unhinged circus performers, living entirely without rules or embarrassment. While humans spend fifty hours a week behind desks just to afford mortgage payments, a monkey wakes up, does a double backflip off a palm tree, and hurls a half-eaten banana directly at a tourist’s face with absolute zero remorse. They are the undisputed grand champions of chaos, trading grooming sessions for stolen sunglasses and managing an entire black-market economy based exclusively on pilfered snacks. Honestly, between their fearless acrobatic antics and utter disregard for personal boundary etiquette, monkeys have clearly figured out the true secret to life.

If mischief were an Olympic sport, monkeys would take home the gold, silver, and bronze—along with the judge's wristwatch. Their favorite pastime is psychological warfare disguised as play: sneaking up on an unsuspecting dog, yanking its tail with lightning speed, and scrambling up a branch just in time to giggle as the dog barks at empty air. They will casually snatch a hat right off a tourist’s head, wear it at a rakish angle, and dangle it just inches out of reach while flashing a wide, toothy grin that screams, "What are you gonna do about it?" Teasing isn't just a habit for monkeys; it’s an art form powered by sheer audacity, lightning reflexes, and an unshakeable joy in pushing everyone else's buttons.

To a monkey, a banana isn’t just fruit—it’s a holy relic, a soulmate, and the absolute center of the known universe. Watch a monkey peel a banana, and you’ll witness a level of tender, delicate reverence that most humans reserve for their own wedding days. But the moment another monkey looks sideways at that golden treasure, the romance vanishes, replaced by pure, unadulterated primal fury. They will guard a single banana with their entire life, clutching it to their chest like a precious heirloom, peeling it with surgical precision, and shoving the entire thing into their mouth in one terrifying, triumphant gulp. It is a legendary love story written in yellow.
