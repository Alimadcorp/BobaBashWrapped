the tale of Orpheus and Eurydice

the story itself:

so orpheus was a musician (and a muse's son). he fell in love with eurydice. they got married. but there was some god who liked eurydice. she was in the forest when he went to her. she ran away from him, towards... a snake. the snake bit her aaand she died.

orpheus was sad. he believed eurydice's life ended before her time. he couldnt accept that she was gone. so he travelled to the underworld to get her back. he made his way through the underworld by singing. everyone liked his song.

he went to hades and persephone. obviously no one can just walk in and take back whoever they want to. thats not how the underworld works. but orpheus played a song with his lyre. it was so heartbreaking and beautiful that even hades was moved.

hades allowed orpheus that, fine, he can take eurydice with him. on ONE CONDITION. he will walk in front. she will walk behind him. and he could not turn around to look at her. if he did, he would lose eurydice forever.

orpheus thanked hades and began his journey. he walked. and walked. and walked. but he could not hear the sound of eurydice's footsteps. what if he had been tricked? and she wasnt even there? and he would step outside the underworld only to see she was left behind. or she got hurt on the way.

a few steps from the exit, he turned around.

there's a quote from ovid's metamorphoses:

'Eurydice, dying now a second time, uttered no complaint against her husband. What was there to complain of, but that she had been loved?'

there are so many interpretations of this story. everytime i tell someone the story for the first time, theyre like: "thats so stupid!" and i get sad and idk how to defend him. it's lowk valid to think that. ig. but the people that say "if i was orpheus, i wouldnt turn around" and it just. no. i dont like that.

but most (not all ofc) people who are familiar with the story have agreed that theres no doubt orpheus loved eurydice. the discussion ive seen most people have is "why did he turn around?". some people say it's BECAUSE he loved eurydice. some people say that yes, he loved eurydice, but the reason he turned around was his doubt (in himself) or fear or smth. i love reading people's different views on this. im too tired to get into my own thoughts.
