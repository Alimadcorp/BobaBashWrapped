Countries and Funfacts


Costa Rica completely abolished its military in 1949 and has lived peacefully without a standing army ever since.

Nauru is the only country in the world without an official capital city.

Liechtenstein sent 80 soldiers to guard the mountain passes against Italy, saw no combat, and returned home with 81 men after an extra person.
Australia is wider than the Moon, measuring nearly 4,000 km across compared to the Moon's 3,400 km diameter.
