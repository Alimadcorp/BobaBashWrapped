Classic Pepperoni Pizza

A crisp golden crust topped with savory tomato sauce, rich mozzarella, and spicy pepperoni curls.

Time: 35 mins | Difficulty: Easy

✨ HOVER TO REVEAL RECIPE ✨

Ingredients:

  • 1 ball of fresh pizza dough (250g)
  • 1/2 cup premium Italian tomato sauce
  • 1.5 cups shredded low-moisture mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 15-20 slices of spicy beef or chicken pepperoni
  • 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
  • Fresh basil leaves and dried oregano for garnishing
  • A pinch of red chili flakes (optional for heat)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat your kitchen oven to its highest setting, ideally 450°F to 500°F (230°C to 260°C), and lightly grease your pizza pan with olive oil.
  2. Place your fresh dough ball on a lightly floured surface. Use your hands to gently press and stretch it out into a 12-inch round circle, keeping the edges slightly thicker for a crunchy crust.
  3. Transfer the stretched dough onto your prepared baking pan. Ladle 1/2 cup of tomato sauce onto the center and spread it outward in a circular motion, leaving a 1/2-inch border clean around the edge.
  4. Evenly sprinkle the grated Parmesan cheese first, followed by a generous, thick layer of shredded mozzarella cheese over all of the sauce.
  5. Arrange your pepperoni slices evenly across the layer of cheese. Drizzle one tablespoon of extra virgin olive oil lightly over the entire pizza, especially on the exposed dough edges.
  6. Place the pan in your hot oven and bake for 12 to 15 minutes, or until the cheese is completely melted, bubbling, and golden-brown, and the outer crust rim looks beautifully crisp.
  7. Carefully slide the pizza out of the oven. Let it cool down for 2 to 3 minutes, garnish with fresh basil or dried oregano, slice it into equal triangles, and serve fresh!

Chicken Dum Biryani

Fragrant basmati rice layered with juicy marinated chicken, slow-cooked with aromatic traditional spices.

Time: 60 mins | Difficulty: Medium

✨ HOVER TO REVEAL RECIPE ✨

Ingredients:

  • 500g Basmati Rice (washed and soaked for 30 minutes)
  • 500g Chicken pieces (bone-in for maximum flavor)
  • 3 tablespoons Shan Sindhi Biryani Masala (adjust for spice preference)
  • 1 cup fresh yogurt (whisked smoothly)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger paste & 1 tablespoon garlic paste
  • 3 medium onions (finely sliced and fried until crispy golden brown)
  • 2 medium tomatoes (chopped cleanly into cubes)
  • 2 medium potatoes (peeled and cut into halves)
  • 3-4 green chilies (whole or slit vertically)
  • 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves & 1/4 cup chopped coriander
  • 4 tablespoons cooking oil or pure ghee

Instructions:

  1. Heat 4 tablespoons of oil or ghee in a large cooking pot. Add the sliced onions and fry them over medium heat until they turn completely crispy and golden brown. Take out a small handful of onions and save them later for garnishing.
  2. Add the chicken pieces, ginger paste, and garlic paste to the pot. Stir-fry over high heat for 5 minutes until the chicken changes color and becomes lightly sealed.
  3. Stir in the chopped tomatoes, potato halves, whisked yogurt, and 3 tablespoons of Shan Sindhi Biryani Masala. Cover the pot with a tight lid and let it simmer on medium-low heat for 15 to 20 minutes until the potatoes are soft and the chicken is fully cooked through.
  4. While the chicken cooks, bring a separate large pot of water to a rolling boil. Add your soaked Basmati rice and cook it until it is about 70% cooked (the grain should still have a slight snap in the middle). Drain the water completely.
  5. Lower the stove heat of your chicken pot. Layer the drained, warm basmati rice evenly over the top of the cooked chicken masala base. Do not stir or mix them together.
  6. Scatter your fresh mint leaves, chopped coriander, green chilies, and the saved golden fried onions evenly across the top layer of rice. Drizzle your saffron-infused milk over the top for color.
  7. Cover the pot tightly with a lid (you can wrap the lid in a clean kitchen towel to seal the steam). Let it cook on ultra-low heat ("Dum") for 10 to 15 minutes until steam builds up and the rice grains are perfectly fluffy and tender. Mix gently from the bottom before serving!

Ultimate Signature Smash Burgers

Crispy, thin smashed beef patties with seared edges, melted American cheese, fresh toppings, and special secret sauce in a toasted bun.

Time: 20 mins | Difficulty: Medium

🍔 HOVER TO REVEAL SECRET FORMULA 🍔

Ingredients:

  • 200g ground beef (80/20 fat blend divided into two balls)
  • 2 slices of yellow American cheese
  • 2 soft potato burger buns
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Secret Sauce: 3 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tbsp ketchup, 1 tsp yellow mustard, 1 tsp sweet pickle relish
  • Toppings: 4 crispy pickle slices, 2 thin slices of fresh tomato, shredded iceberg lettuce, 2 thin rings of red onion

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the secret sauce in a small bowl by mixing the mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard, and sweet pickle relish until smooth. Set aside.
  2. Slice the potato buns in half and spread butter on the cut sides. Toast them on a warm pan until golden brown and set aside.
  3. Heat a heavy cast-iron skillet or flat griddle on high heat until it gets smoking hot.
  4. Place the beef balls onto the dry, hot skillet and press down immediately using a heavy flat burger press or spatula until completely flat and thin.
  5. Season the beef patties generously with salt and black pepper. Cook undisturbed for 2 minutes until the bottom edges are dark brown and very crispy.
  6. Carefully scrape and flip the patties over. Immediately place a yellow American cheese slice on each patty and cook for 1 more minute until melted.
  7. Spread a generous layer of the secret sauce on the bottom bun. Layer the shredded lettuce, tomato slices, onion rings, and pickles on top.
  8. Stack the two cheesy smashed beef patties on top of the fresh vegetable layer, cover with the top bun, and enjoy immediately!

Decadent New York Cheesecake

A rich, dense, and creamy baked cheesecake slice sitting on a sweet, buttery graham cracker crust.

Time: 50 mins | Difficulty: Hard

🍰 HOVER TO REVEAL SECRET FORMULA 🍰

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 cups crushed graham crackers or biscuits
  • 4 tablespoons melted butter
  • 3 packs (total 600g) cream cheese at room temperature
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions:

  1. Mix the biscuit crumbs with melted butter and press firmly into the base of a springform pan.
  2. Beat the cream cheese and sugar together in a large mixing bowl until perfectly smooth and creamy.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla extract, blending gently on low speed.
  4. Pour the filling over the crust base and bake at 325°F (160°C) for 45 minutes until the center sets.

Ooey Gooey Fudge Brownies

Rich, dense chocolate brownies featuring crisp crinkle tops and sweet, chewy fudge centers.

Time: 25 mins | Difficulty: Easy

🍫 HOVER TO REVEAL SECRET FORMULA 🍫

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup melted unsalted butter
  • 2 cups white sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Instructions:

  1. Preheat your kitchen oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a baking pan.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the melted butter, sugar, and eggs until smooth.
  3. Stir in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt gently until a thick batter forms.
  4. Pour the mixture evenly into the pan and bake for 20 to 22 minutes.

Sweet Chili Wings

Crispy fried chicken wings tossed in a sweet, sticky, and tangy Asian-style sweet chili sauce glaze.

Time: 30 mins | Difficulty: Easy

🍗 HOVER TO REVEAL SECRET FORMULA 🍗

Ingredients:

  • 500g chicken wings (split into flats and drumettes)
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch powder
  • 1/2 cup sweet chili sauce
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Pat the chicken wings completely dry with a paper towel and coat them heavily in cornstarch.
  2. Deep fry the wings in hot oil at 375°F for 12 to 15 minutes until golden and crunchy.
  3. In a separate large bowl, combine the sweet chili sauce and soy sauce together.
  4. Toss the hot fried chicken wings instantly in the sauce mix and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Garlic Parmesan Wings

Crispy chicken wings tossed in rich melted butter, aromatic minced garlic, and sharp Parmesan cheese.

Time: 30 mins | Difficulty: Easy

🍗 HOVER TO REVEAL SECRET FORMULA 🍗

Ingredients:

  • 500g chicken wings (split into flats and drumettes)
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch powder
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter (melted)
  • 3 cloves garlic (very finely minced)
  • 1/2 cup finely grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions:

  1. Pat chicken wings completely dry with a paper towel and coat them heavily in cornstarch powder.
  2. Deep fry the wings in hot cooking oil at 375°F for 12 to 15 minutes until golden and crispy.
  3. Melt butter in a small pan, add minced garlic, and cook gently for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Place hot fried wings in a bowl, pour garlic butter over them, add Parmesan cheese, and toss.

Chili Cheese Fries

Crispy golden French fries loaded with warm spicy minced beef chili and a rich blanket of melted cheddar.

Time: 25 mins | Difficulty: Easy

🍟 HOVER TO REVEAL SECRET FORMULA 🍟

Ingredients:

  • 1 large bag of frozen French fries
  • 1 cup cooked minced beef chili sauce
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1/4 cup sliced jalapeno peppers
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh chives

Instructions:

  1. Bake or deep fry the French fries according to the package directions until extra crispy.
  2. Place the hot cooked fries onto a large heat-safe serving plate or oven dish.
  3. Pour the warm minced beef chili sauce evenly over the top of the fries.
  4. Scatter the cheddar cheese and jalapeno slices on top, then broil in the oven for 2 minutes to melt.
